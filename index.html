ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE

Odisha Outbreak

You wake up in your room in Odisha, India. Overnight the outbreak spread: the people outside have turned into zombies. With only a small knife from your room, you must survive the infested streets, find a gun, and link up with the few survivors holding out at a research center. They are racing to build a cure. Gather the ingredients they need, travel north to the medical facility, deliver the cure to the lead scientist, then board the helicopter to spread it across the world.
